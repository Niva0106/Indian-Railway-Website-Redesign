INTRODUCTION

A Railway Zone is a regional administrative unit of Indian Railways. These zones facilitate:

*Better operational management
*Effective train scheduling
*Smooth maintenance and service operations
*Regional governance and revenue tracking

India boasts one of the largest and busiest railway networks in the world, managed by the Indian Railways, which is owned and operated by the Ministry of Railways, Government of India. To ensure efficient administration and smooth functioning, the railway network is divided into 18 zones. Each zone oversees operations in a particular geographical area and comprises several divisions under it.
